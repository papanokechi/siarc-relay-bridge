                                      Introduction

      The Painleve transcendents and their generalizations have aroused much
interest among mathematicians and mathematical physicists in recent years
([l]-[8]). Yet their properties as special functions still remain almost unex-
plored. For example, the problem of determining their behavior at the fixed
critical singularities is left open, except for a few special cases ([3] [4]). In the
present paper we treat this problem for the following three kinds of Painleve
equations at the fixed critical point i = 0 :
