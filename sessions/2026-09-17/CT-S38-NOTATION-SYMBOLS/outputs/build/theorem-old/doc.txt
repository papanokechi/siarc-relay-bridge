Remark 6 (Pincherle interpretation of (H)). Pincherle’s theorem (standard; see e.g. Gautschi,
Computational aspects of three-term recurrence relations, SIAM Rev. 9 (1967), and Lorentzen–
Waadeland, Continued Fractions with Applications, Ch. IV) states that the continued fraction
associated with a three-term recurrence converges if and only if the recurrence possesses a minimal
solution, Q
i.e. a solution yn with yn /zn → 0 for every linearly independent solution zn .
Here k≤n b(k) is, up to a constant, the natural “dominant” scale. Hypothesis (H) says precisely
that Qn is not the minimal solution of (2): the minimal solution Pn satisfies Pn /Bn → 0, so a
solution with Qn /Bn → r∞ ̸= 0 is dominant. Equivalently, (H) fails exactly when the chosen initial
data (Q−1 , Q0 ) = (0, 1) happens to land on the one-dimensional minimal subspace. Since that
subspace has codimension one, (H) is generic; Proposition 3 makes it automatic in the positive
case. Label: structural — Pincherle’s theorem is cited as standard and the identification of Bn
with the dominant scale is asserted here only for interpretation; nothing below depends on this
remark.

Scope remark

Stated plainly, and deliberately restrictive:
1. The theorem gives the radius and the leading growth only. It locates the singularity
of B(w) nearest the origin and gives the leading coefficient asymptotics of Qn /Γ(dn + 1). It
does not determine the analytic type of the Borel singularity at ξ0 : whether it is a pure branch
point of exponent −(s + 1), whether logarithms occur, and what the subleading germ looks
like are all outside the statement.

2. It reads only βd and βd−1 . The exponent s depends on no other coefficient, and the radius
depends only on βd . TheQlower coefficients β0 , . . . , βd−2 enter (15) only through the scalar
prefactor K (via r∞ and i Γ(1 − ri )). Consequently any test that inspects only the radius
or only the exponent is insensitive to β0 , . . . , βd−2 and will return the same verdict on an
entire family of b’s. This is the structural explanation of a vacuity phenomenon of exactly
this shape.
