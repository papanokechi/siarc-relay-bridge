## 2. Why V3 is ill-posed as written

V3's stated reasoning was:

> the system without it is a strictly weaker condition, so a degree that does
> not move would show the observable cannot see that equation

**That inference is invalid.** A strictly weaker generating set can define the
same variety, if the dropped polynomial already lies in the **radical** of the
ideal generated by the others. So V3 cannot separate two causes with opposite
meanings:

- **(R) redundancy** — `b1 = 0` is *implied* by the five coefficient equations
  on the saturated locus. A fact about the system. The instrument is fine and
  the arm asked the wrong question.
- **(I) insensitivity** — the degree genuinely cannot see a generator being
  removed. The instrument is broken and every degree it reports is void.

This is the **same defect class as `S23-07`**: an arm written in the expectation
of one outcome, which then cannot distinguish an insensitive instrument from a
legitimate agreement. Noted as a recurrence of a known class, not as a new one.

It is worth being explicit that V3 was authored *with* `S23-07` in mind and
still reproduced its shape. The lesson that generalises: **a sensitivity arm
must be paired with a positive control on the same observable**, because
"the observable did not move" is never self-interpreting.

## 3. The discriminating tests, fixed before running

- **V3a — radical ideal membership.** Is `b1` in
  `radical(<5 coefficient equations, Rabinowitsch>)`? Decided **exactly**: `b1`
  is in the radical **iff** adjoining `1 − v·b1` yields the **unit ideal**.
  - *Control on V3a, required:* a polynomial known **not** to vanish on the
    variety — `theta_inf` itself, whose roots are `−2 ± √3/9` — must be reported
    as **NOT** in the radical. Without this arm, a membership test that
    accepted everything would look like a pass.
- **V3b — positive sensitivity control.** Drop **each** coefficient equation in
  turn, keeping `b1 = 0`. If the degree moves for **any** drop, the observable
  demonstrably **can** see a generator being removed, excluding cause (I).

## 4. Verdict rule, fixed before running

Cause **(R)** is established **only if V3a returns YES *and* V3b moves for at
least one drop**. Either alone is insufficient:

- V3a alone shows redundancy but not that the instrument works;
- V3b alone shows the instrument works but not why this particular drop was
  invisible.

If **V3b does not move for any drop**, cause **(I)** holds, the instrument is
insensitive, and **every degree reported in this session is VOID** — including
the degree-2 main result. That is the outcome that would sink N1, and it is
stated here before it is known.

If the result is inconclusive, take the `PREREG.md` §6 fallback unchanged.
