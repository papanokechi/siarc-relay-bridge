## 2. Hypothesis

**H.** The ideal generated by the five coefficient-matching equations together
with the no-apparent-singularity condition `b1 = 0`, in the unknowns
`(p, q, t, theta_inf)`, **saturated at `q(q-1) != 0`**, is zero-dimensional of
degree exactly **2** over `K = Q(i*sqrt11)`.

If H holds, the variety has **at most** two points counted with multiplicity.
Two distinct points are already known. Therefore the solution set is exactly
those two, and `S16-12`'s "both sign branches" is **PROVEN** rather than
solver-asserted.

**Saturation is not optional.** `a`, `c`, `b1` and `b0` carry denominators `q`
and `q-1`, and the equations were formed from **numerators**. Any component of
the numerator variety lying inside `q = 0` or `q = 1` is an artefact of
clearing denominators and is not a solution of the original problem. It must be
removed by saturation, not by inspection.

## 5. Vacuity pre-check — run BEFORE the result is consulted

The existing P0 rule asks whether the observable moves when the data the
hypothesis is about moves. Here the data is the equation set and the observable
is the degree.

- **V1 (positive control).** Both recorded solutions must satisfy **every**
  generator **exactly** (symbolic zero, not numeric smallness). If they do not,
  the transcription of the construction is wrong. *This is the arm that catches
  a mistranscribed equation, which would otherwise produce a confident and
  entirely false degree.*
- **V2 (negative control).** A deliberately perturbed point must **not** satisfy
  the generators. Guards against a generator set that is identically zero, which
  would make every point a solution and the degree meaningless.
- **V3 (sensitivity).** **Drop** the `b1 = 0` equation and recompute. The degree
  must **change** — the system without it is a strictly weaker condition, so a
  degree that does not move would show the observable cannot see that equation,
  and the whole computation would be void. **If V3 does not move, halt.**
- **V4 (saturation is doing work).** Compute the degree **without** the
  Rabinowitsch variable. If it is unchanged, saturation removed nothing and the
  claim that it was needed is unsupported; record that, rather than continuing
  to assert saturation mattered. *This arm can legitimately pass either way; it
  is recorded as a finding, not a gate.*

**Basis-degeneracy pre-check (Amendment A):** *not applicable, and stated rather
than skipped silently.* This is not a search over a basis of my own
construction; it is an elimination on an ideal whose generators are forced by
the matching problem. There is no space of my own choosing whose internal
relations could absorb the target.
