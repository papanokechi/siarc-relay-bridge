## 1. The title advertises as a contribution what the notice attributes away

The published title of record 22850681 is, verbatim:

> Channel Theory for Polynomial Continued Fractions: Asymptotic Channels, the
> xi_0 = 2/sqrt(beta_2) Identity, and a Bridge Conjecture

The v1.7 notice attributes that identity to **Perron-Kreuser plus Pincherle**
(7 and 10 mentions respectively in the published description). The title still
names it as one of three headline contributions. Title and text therefore
disagree about what is new.

**Ruling, for v1.8:** section 0 should carry the line that the identity named
in the title is classical, and that the **Bridge Conjecture is the record's
remaining conjectural content**.

The title itself is unchanged by this; the correction goes in the text, which
is how every prior version in this record handled an erratum.

## 2. Observed while checking the above, not previously reported

also the one the notice never names. The remaining conjectural content is
advertised in the title and absent from the text. That strengthens the ruling
in section 1 rather than complicating it: section 0's line has to introduce the
Bridge Conjecture, not merely relabel it.
