Subject: MATH-D-26-00523: author's corrections to four statements

Dear Professor Jimbo,

I am writing about my submission MATH-D-26-00523, "An explicit exponential-period representation of the V_quad connection coefficient", currently under review.

Since submitting, I have found statements in the manuscript that claim more than the paper establishes. I would rather bring them to your attention now than have a referee find them. None affects the main results: Theorem 1.1, Theorem 1.2, and Corollaries 1.3 and 1.4 do not use any of the statements below.

1. Surface identification. The abstract, Sections 1.1, 1.2 and 7.1, and my cover letter state as fact that V_quad is a Painlevé V transcendent on the Sakai surface D5^(1). That identification is conjectural (Conjecture 4.1 of the companion paper [1]), and the companion's v1.3 correction notice revises the derivation of the associated Painlevé parameters quoted in Section 1.1. These passages should state the identification as conjectural.

2. Section 7.1 states that the paper "completes part (ii)(a) for d = 2". This overclaims. The paper exhibits the explicit exponential period for the single representative V_quad, and it is an instance of part (ii)(a) only conditionally on the conjectured surface identification in item 1. It does not establish part (ii)(a) for the stratum.

3. Theorem 1.2 is stated as "proved twice". The Kovacic case-elimination is complete. The structural argument, as written, shows only that the Galois group contains a Borel subgroup: the Stokes unipotent lies in a root group of the exponential torus, and a torus with one root unipotent generates a Borel subgroup, not SL_2. Combined with the exclusion of reducibility (Kovacic Case 1) it does give SL_2(C), but it is then not an independent second proof. Theorem 1.2 stands; the claim of two independent proofs does not.

4. [If the log check finds no logarithms:] Proposition 2.4 and Lemma 2.7 infer that xi = 0 is an apparent singularity from its integer exponents alone, which does not suffice in general. I have since verified directly that the Frobenius solutions at xi = 0 carry no logarithmic terms, so the conclusion stands; the argument needs this added.
[If logarithms are found:] Proposition 2.4, Lemma 2.7, Table 1 and Remark 3.4 describe xi = 0 as an apparent singularity, inferred from its integer exponents. A direct Frobenius computation shows logarithmic terms, so the point is not apparent. The main identity is unaffected, since B-hat is holomorphic at 0 on the principal sheet and the cycle does not approach it, but these statements must be corrected.

I would be glad to supply a revised manuscript, or a list of corrections for the referees, whichever you prefer.
