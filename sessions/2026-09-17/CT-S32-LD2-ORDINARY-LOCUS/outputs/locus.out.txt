general theta-coefficients at d = 2:
  c_0(z) = alpha0*z**2 + 2*alpha1*z**2 + 4*alpha2*z**2 + beta0*z + beta1*z + beta2*z - 1
  c_1(z) = z*(alpha1*z + 4*alpha2*z + beta1 + 2*beta2)
  c_2(z) = z*(alpha2*z + beta2)
  so c_0(z) = a(2) z^2 + b(1) z - 1  :  b(1) and a(2) are the only way
  the coefficients enter c_0, and the lone -1 fixes ord_0 c_0 = 0.
