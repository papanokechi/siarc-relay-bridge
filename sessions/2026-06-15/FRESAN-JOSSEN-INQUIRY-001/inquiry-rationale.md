## Why this specific question

The V_quad period paper (VQUAD-PERIODREP-PAPER-001) reaches an explicit exponential-
period representation and then, in §6, makes a **transcendence** statement that rests
on **two** layers of conditionality:

1. the Fresán–Jossen **period conjecture** itself (Conjecture 1.3.2, `trdeg = dim G_M`);
2. a **comparison** identifying the paper's *differential* (Picard–Vessiot) Galois data
   with the *motivic* Galois group `G_M` — the **G-MOTGALOIS gap**.

Layer 1 is a famous open conjecture; nothing an email can change. **Layer 2 is the
soft spot we can actually move.** FJ's own framework keeps the Picard–Vessiot group
and `G_M` distinct (FJ-axioms §2.5 #6). The paper currently bridges them heuristically.
The single highest-value clarification is therefore: *for a motive of V_quad's exact
shape, is that bridge a theorem, a known simplification, or genuinely open?* That is
the question the email asks — nothing broader.

## Why this framing (Frame C, not Frame A)

- **Frame A** ("Is the motivic-Galois comparison true?") invites a one-line "that is the
  open conjecture" and ends the exchange.
- **Frame C** ("Does theorem [Ex. 1.1.4 / Ex. 1.1.5 / Conj. 1.3.2 machinery] **apply to
  this case**?") gives Fresán concrete objects to react to and a low-cost productive
  reply ("yes, via …", "no, because rigidity fails", "almost, but check …"). It mirrors
  the inquiry pattern that previously produced a substantive, citable exchange.
- The email leads with **firmly established** data (`SL(2)` by exact Kovacic; the
  structural `G_V`) and is explicit that the **motivic** step is what it is asking about
  — so it cannot be read as claiming the comparison.

## Why we do NOT overclaim the `L_V` Galois identification (STAGE 2.2)

`G_V` is **structurally** pinned (a `𝔾_m` from the irrational exponent `−1+√3/9`, plus
an `SL(2)`-dual irregular block with non-trivial Stokes) but was **not** reduced to a
single named algebraic group (no Hrushovski/`DEtools` tool in the sympy-only
environment; VQUAD-003 HALT GATE 3 escape clause). The email therefore states the
`L_V` group **structurally**, and pins only `G(L_φ)=SL(2)` as exact. This keeps the
inquiry honest: we ask the comparison question *given* `SL(2)` + the *structural* `G_V`,
not given a claimed single-name `G_V`.
