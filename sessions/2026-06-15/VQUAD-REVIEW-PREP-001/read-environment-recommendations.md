# Read-environment recommendations — VQUAD-PERIODREP-PAPER-001 cold read

Practical guidance for the operator's cold-read pass. The goal is to spend the
session on the *mathematics*, not on logistics or tooling.

## Setup

- **Read the PDF, not the markdown source.** Open
  `sessions/2026-06-15/VQUAD-PERIODREP-PAPER-001/latex/vquad-periodrep-paper.pdf`
  (23 pp). Do not split attention between `vquad-periodrep-paper.md` and the PDF —
  the PDF is the artifact a referee will see, and the typeset layout (paragraph
  length, equation breaks, page turns) is exactly what Lecerf Criterion 1 judges.
- **Print or single-screen.** Either print it, or put it full-screen on one
  monitor. Avoid a half-width window beside the checklist; page-turn rhythm and
  paragraph density read differently at full size.
- **One uninterrupted session, 2–3 hours minimum.** A cold read is a single
  continuous judgement; fragmenting it across days resets the "fresh reader"
  signal the read is meant to capture.

## File-safety lesson (do this first)

- **Do NOT read or annotate from a OneDrive Files-On-Demand path.** A prior
  session had OneDrive dehydrate and delete files mid-work. The bridge slot is
  already on a local non-OneDrive path (`C:\LocalWork\siarc-relay-bridge\...`),
  which is safe — keep it there.
- If you copy the PDF elsewhere to annotate, copy it to another **local**
  (non-OneDrive, non-dehydrating) path first. Do not annotate a cloud-synced copy
  that may dehydrate between save and reopen.

## How to read

1. **Read STRAIGHT THROUGH first.** Resist the urge to fix issues as you find
   them. On the first pass you are a reader, not an editor — note reactions in
   `cold-read-checklist.md` (or in the margins) but keep moving.
2. **Only on a second pass, fix or flag.** After the full read, return to the
   flagged spots and decide: fix now, or flag for the corrections slot.
3. **Read `self-review.md` AFTER your own pass — not before.** Reading the
   agent's self-review first primes you to look for what it already found, which
   contaminates the independent signal. Read fresh, record your own findings,
   *then* open `self-review.md` and fill in the "Comparison to agent's
   self-review" section. The delta (what each of you missed) is itself a signal.

## Capturing reactions

- Use `cold-read-checklist.md` as the structured capture surface. Its fill-in
  fields are deliberately open-ended; the checkboxes mark decisions. It records
  *where* you reacted without telling you *what* to think.
- For the 6 pre-flagged open items, the pre-committed operator decisions are
  consolidated in `open-items-decisions.md` — glance at it before the read so you
  know which items are already settled (LOW-1 deferred; MED-1/2 conservative;
  MED-3 strengthen) and which need your in-read judgement (LOW-2, LOW-3, and the
  *adequacy* call on MED-3).

## After the read

- Fill in the "Overall verdict" and "Next actions" in the checklist.
- Do **not** apply edits in the paper slot during this prep stage. Edits belong
  in the next downstream slot (VQUAD-PAPER-CORRECTIONS-001), opened by the
  operator after the read so the corrections scope is informed by what the read
  actually found.
