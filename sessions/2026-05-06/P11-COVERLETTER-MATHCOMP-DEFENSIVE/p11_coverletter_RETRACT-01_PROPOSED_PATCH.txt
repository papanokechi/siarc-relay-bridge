THE PROBLEM IN ONE LINE
-----------------------
Paragraph 2 argues that Mathematics of Computation is the right venue
BECAUSE the work is "at certified precision, with a machine-checkable
certificate."  MANIFEST-01 retracted that: dps=500 covers 50 families,
not 400,093, and the pipeline used mpmath floats with no interval
enclosure.  So the retracted claim is not a stray figure in the letter —
it is the stated reason for choosing the journal.  Deleting the number
leaves the venue argument with no support.  This is a rewrite.

PATCH 1 of 3  —  lines 26-29, the venue argument  [LOAD-BEARING]
----------------------------------------------------------------
OLD:
  I believe Mathematics of Computation is the appropriate venue
  because the central contribution is computational: an algorithmic
  classification of an explicit arithmetic family at certified
  precision, with a machine-checkable certificate.

NEW:
  I believe Mathematics of Computation is the appropriate venue
  because the central contribution is computational: an exhaustive
  algorithmic classification of an explicit arithmetic family,
  carried out at high working precision (dps = 300-500) and shipped
  with a complete, independently re-runnable computation ledger.

Rationale: "exhaustive" and "independently re-runnable" are true, are
defensible to a referee, and are the qualities Math. Comp. actually
selects for.  "Certified" is not available to us and would not survive
a referee asking to see the enclosure.


PATCH 2 of 3  —  lines 45-51, the reproducibility paragraph
-----------------------------------------------------------
OLD:
  ... each tagged with its working precision (dps = 300-500), the
  script that produced it (f1_base_computation.py), and a SHA-256
  output hash.  The machine-checkable certificate is supplementary
  file f1_base_certificate.json.

NEW:
  ... each tagged with its working precision, the script that
  produced it (f1_base_computation.py), and a SHA-256 output hash.
  The computation ledger is supplementary file
  f1_base_certificate.json.  We state the precision regime
  explicitly: of the Desert stratum, 2,000 families were confirmed
  at dps = 300 and 50 at dps = 500.  The classification is a
  high-precision floating-point computation and does not carry
  interval enclosures; we make no rigorous-certification claim for
  it.

Rationale: this is the strongest form of the disclosure.  Stating the
limitation ourselves is worth more to a referee than having it found,
and it costs nothing the paper actually had.  Note the filename
f1_base_certificate.json is left alone — renaming a supplementary
artifact would break the claim ledger's own hashes.
